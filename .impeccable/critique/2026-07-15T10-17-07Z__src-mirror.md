## Anti-Patterns Verdict

**LLM assessment**: Passes the hard bans — no side-stripe accents, no gradient text, no glassmorphism, no hero-metric template. The systemic slop tell is **emoji as the entire icon system** (⏱ 🎯 👁 🙈 📅 🗓️ 💾 ↺ 📊 📥 💰 🏆 🛌 ⏸ 🔒 📍), which contradicts the declared brand "confiável, discreto, preciso": OS-dependent rendering, playful connotations (🙈 for privacy), and two different calendar glyphs for the same action. Wall-to-wall 9–10px uppercase section labels push it toward template-y rather than instrument-grade.

**Deterministic scan**: 0 findings across `src/mirror` and `src/shared` (exit 0, verified live via smoke test; `.js` template literals confirmed in scan scope). Genuine true negative for its rule set — but note the detector's `low-contrast` rule could not catch the token-composed failures Assessment A verified by hand (preset pill 3.88:1; hint colors ~1.6:1 in light theme), because the color pairs are assembled across files at runtime.

**Visual overlays**: browser step skipped — no browser automation tool exposed; target is a userscript overlay on an authenticated third-party host page. Fallback signal: source-verified contrast math + CSS inspection.

## Overall Impression

The information architecture genuinely delivers "resposta antes da pergunta" — the assistant card with the 30px next-punch time and legal window is first in the body, and the enforcement badges are causally honest. What undermines trust is the interaction layer: every interaction rebuilds the whole panel via `innerHTML`, core controls are keyboard-unreachable, and the scariest flow (editing punches) never says it's a local simulation. The single biggest opportunity: make the panel feel as precise to *operate* as it is to *read*.

## What's Working

1. **"Resposta antes da pergunta" is implemented, not just claimed** — `.ahg-as-next-time` hero + legal window opens the body (`assistant-card.js:97-108`, `css-injector.js:458`); done/next/planned schedule states make the day scannable in one fixation.
2. **Honest, auditable enforcement** — `capExit` (`assistant-engine.js:104-119`) attributes which BR rule bound the cap; the badge the user sees is causally true. Rare rigor.
3. **Real a11y infrastructure in CSS** — centralized `:focus-visible` ring (15 selectors, `css-injector.js:63-81`), `prefers-reduced-motion` block, tokenized z-index scale, light-theme semantics verified ≥4.5:1.

## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] Full `innerHTML` re-render on every interaction destroys state** (`panel-renderer.js:186,192,216,235,243`). Focus evaporates, scroll resets, open `<details>` snaps shut — the tool punishes exploration. **Fix**: patch targeted nodes, or persist/restore `details.open`, scrollTop, and active element. **Command**: /impeccable harden
2. **[P1] Core controls keyboard-unreachable** — minimize is a `<span>` (`panel-renderer.js:68`); "Horas realizadas"/"Exportar CSV" are click-handled `<div>`s (`:132-133`, the `:focus-visible` rule for them is dead code); modal has no `role="dialog"`, no Esc, no focus trap (`details-modal.js:66-91`); drag is mousedown-only. **Fix**: `<button>` semantics, dialog role + Esc + trap, pointer events. **Command**: /impeccable audit → harden
3. **[P1] Verified AA contrast failures** — `.ahg-as-preset.active` white on `#7a6cff` = 3.88:1 at 10px/700 (`css-injector.js:456`); `renderHint` hard-codes `#ff8888/#ffd08a/#22c55e` (`punch-editor.js:99`) — `#ffd08a` on white ≈1.6:1 in light theme, on exactly the "turno > 6h" warning row. **Fix**: use theme tokens; darken active-preset fill. **Command**: /impeccable colorize (tokens) or polish
4. **[P2] Destructive actions without confirmation or undo** — "↺ Reset" persists on one click (`punch-editor.js:242-249`); closing editor discards edits silently (`:294-299`); toasts lack undo and `aria-live` (`ui-components.js:123-132`). **Fix**: undo in toast, dirty-check on close. **Command**: /impeccable harden
5. **[P2] Unstyled classes shipped to the calendar** — `.ahg-day-violations` + modifiers (`calendar-decorator.js:44-48`), base `.ahg-day-total-rounded`, `.ahg-btn-sm` (6 uses) have no CSS rules anywhere. Violation codes render as raw unpositioned text in host calendar cells. **Fix**: add rules or delete vestigial classes. **Command**: /impeccable polish

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (Power User)**: Preset toggle rebuilds the panel and closes his open "Hoje em detalhe". Two saldo lines ("Saldo semanal" `panel-renderer.js:96` vs "Saldo fechado da semana" `assistant-card.js:169`) force reconciliation — which one does "Saída ideal" compensate? No copy-to-clipboard on the hero time, though the logger has copy targets.

**Sam (Screen reader / keyboard)**: Cannot minimize, cannot open monthly details, cannot export CSV, cannot close the modal, cannot drag. Privacy toggle's accessible name is the emoji ("see-no-evil monkey"). Toasts never announced (no live region). Punch-editor `render()` drops focus after confirm/remove.

**Riley (Stress tester)**: 6-punch day → assistant schedule silently truncates to 4 with mislabeled steps while "Batidas hoje" says 6 (`assistant-engine.js:214-217`). Privacy mode masks the *text* row "Situação" as `••:••` — a status word becomes a fake time. Multi-instance punch editors share one z-index; earlier one can't be raised. `quickCalendarProvider: 'both'` → up to 6 tap targets of ~11px emoji.

## Minor Observations

- Touch targets broadly below WCAG 2.2's 24px floor (preset pills ~22px, `.ahg-as-cal` ~15px, request badge 16px).
- DESIGN.md promises modal "overlay escuro"; `details-modal.js` renders no backdrop — outside-click close never fires as backdrop dismissal.
- Calendar edit affordance is hover-revealed (`opacity:0`) — undiscoverable on touch.
- CSV export has no success feedback; the toast pattern exists one file away.
- "Saída ideal" computed by two different engines (assistant vs guidance/calculator) — could disagree.
- Preset buttons lack `aria-pressed`.
- "Hora extra deve ser aprovada pela liderança" (`compliance-engine.js:163`) is the one message framed as policing, violating PRODUCT.md principle 5.
- Before the first punch, the hero shows the current minute as "next punch" — reads as a broken clock, not "pode bater agora" (`assistant-engine.js:124`).

## Questions to Consider

1. If the punch editor is a local simulation, why does it look like a time-card editor? Would renaming "Salvar" to "Simular" plus one line of copy ("nada é enviado ao Ahgora") do more for trust than everything else here combined?
2. Before the first punch, is a 30px clock of *now* the instrument-precision answer — or is that the moment to show the interjornada window and earliest legal entry?
3. Two saldos, one promise ("números exatos, nunca aproximações"). Which one is *the* number, and does the other belong behind the details disclosure?
