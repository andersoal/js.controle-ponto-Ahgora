## Priority Issues

1. **[P1] "Atenção" row renders punch counts through a time formatter** — `panel-renderer.js:62`: `fmtHour(x.count)` renders 3 punches as "00:03", and the day's date is discarded. Fabricated-looking data in a product branded "preciso". **Fix**: `DD/MM (N batidas)` + make row clickable to highlight the days. **Command**: /impeccable harden (data correctness)
2. **[P1] Core controls mouse-only; focus styles are dead code** — minimize span, modal-close span, clickable divs; no Esc; every toggle rebuilds via innerHTML dropping focus to `<body>`. **Fix**: real buttons, dialog role + trap + Esc, focus restoration after re-render. **Command**: /impeccable audit → harden
3. **[P1] Local-simulation nature under-communicated** — toast "salvas.", 9px "ajustado localmente", English `mirror`/`local` tags, lock icon next to a functioning trash button. **Fix**: persistent "Simulação local: nada é enviado ao Ahgora" banner, toast copy "salvas localmente", tags "oficial"/"ajuste local", confirm on Reset. **Command**: /impeccable clarify
4. **[P2] Opacity-dimming breaks AA** — `.ahg-as-step.done` and `.ahg-week-day.future` at `opacity:.55` → labels ≈2.0–2.3:1. **Fix**: dedicated dimmed color tokens ≥4.5:1, de-emphasize via weight instead. **Command**: /impeccable polish
5. **[P2] Punch-edit entry point invisible** — `opacity:0` until cell hover; focus ring can outline an invisible button. **Fix**: resting ghost visibility (~.35) + `opacity:1` on `:focus-visible`. **Command**: /impeccable polish
